Three ways to copy a page as Markdown:

  1. Click the extension icon in the toolbar, then Copy this page. Pin it for one-click access (puzzle icon → pin).
  2. Press Alt+Shift+C on any page.
  3. Right-click a page or selected text → Copy page for AI.

Then paste into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. Pick a prompt template in the popup if you want the content wrapped in “Summarize”, “Explain” or your own instructions.
