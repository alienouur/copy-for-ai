Three ways to get the answers on a page:

  1. Click the extension icon in the toolbar – the side panel opens next to the page. Pin it for one-click access (puzzle icon → pin). Then click Solve this page.
  2. Press Alt+Shift+S on any page.
  3. Right-click a page or selected text → Solve with Copy for AI.

The panel stays open while you read, scroll or reload. Ask follow-ups (“why?”, “explain #3”) in the box at the bottom; tick Answer only when you just want the result. On the first solve, Chrome may ask to allow access to all sites – accept so the panel keeps working after a reload.

Copy as Markdown

The classic tools are still there under Copy as Markdown in the panel: press Alt+Shift+C on any article, then paste into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini.
