FAQ

What kind of questions can it solve?

Anything written on a web page: multiple choice, short answer, math and physics problems, chemistry, code, grammar, reading comprehension. If the page is a PDF or an image, the screenshot is analysed instead.

Does the extension send my pages anywhere?

Only when you click Solve. The page text and, if enabled, a screenshot of the visible area are sent to our server and processed by Google Gemini to produce the answer. They are not stored. Copying as Markdown stays entirely in your browser. See the privacy policy.

Do I need my own API key or account?

No. Install and click – no sign-up. Pro is unlocked with a license key you receive right after checkout.

Can I use Pro on multiple computers?

Yes. Activate your key in every browser you use; it syncs with your Chrome profile.

How do I cancel?

Any time from the Stripe receipt email (“Manage subscription”) or by emailing us. You keep Pro until the end of the paid month.
